# Hi, I'm Young

I'm a software engineer with nine years of shipping consumer apps and the backends behind them. I started in native iOS, moved through React Native and the web, and these days most of my work is full stack with AI agents in the middle of it.

Some things I've built:

- **[Ancher](https://ancher.ai/)** — an AI knowledge workspace. I'm the mobile engineer ([App Store](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6759999347) · [Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=one.streamify.app)) and I work on the agent side: the MCP server, retrieval, and the evaluations that keep answers honest.
- **[PlanFlow Meter](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6751126870)** — a planning app built around interactive widgets, with a sync engine I wrote to keep the app and widgets consistent under concurrent writes.
- **[AI Emoji Converter](https://apps.apple.com/app/id6747648089)** — an on-device model I trained myself, converted to Core ML, running at 60 FPS fully offline.
- At Amazing Tech I led three apps with over a million users combined from Objective-C to Swift without pausing releases. At Pagoda, China's largest fruit retailer, I built the real-time delivery tracking for an app with 10M+ users.
